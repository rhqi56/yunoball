1. System Overview

The JapanTrip2026 Live Monitoring System is a network-based video surveillance and monitoring platform developed for educational and authorized security purposes. Access is strictly controlled and logged.

⚠ This system is protected under Republic Act No. 10173 — Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Philippines).
2. Authorized Use Only

Access is restricted to authorized personnel only. By proceeding, you confirm that:

  • You are an authorized user with valid credentials.
  • You will not share your credentials with any third party.
  • You will not attempt unauthorized access to any feature.
3. Security & Access Monitoring

This System actively monitors all login attempts:

  • All failed login attempts are logged and flagged to Administrators.
  • Accounts are suspended after 3 consecutive failed attempts.
  • IP addresses and session timestamps are recorded for audit purposes.
4. Prohibited Activities
  • Attempting to bypass authentication or lockout mechanisms.
  • Accessing camera feeds without authorization.
  • Sharing or publishing any video data captured through this system.
